Advancing Animal Health & Productivity

AVIVET Animal Health Pvt Ltd. is a research-driven veterinary healthcare company headquartered in Hyderabad, delivering specialized nutrition, therapeutics, and biosecurity solutions across India.

Our Guiding Purpose

Empowering Farmers with Scientifically Proven Veterinary Formulations

At AVIVET, we believe sustainable farming starts with healthier animals. Our mission is to bridge the gap between high-end veterinary biochemistry and real-world farm economics.

Whether managing a 100,000-bird commercial layer facility, an intensive broiler integration, or a high-yielding dairy herd, our formulations are engineered to maximize feed conversion, eliminate deficiency diseases, and safeguard flock longevity.

Our Vision

To become India's most trusted veterinary healthcare brand through zero-compromise product efficacy and ethical partnership.

Our Mission

Delivering bio-available, stable, and cost-effective animal health solutions that increase farmer profitability every single season.

Quality & Manufacturing Excellence

Our 4-Stage Quality Assurance Pipeline

Every single batch of AVIVET products undergoes strict analytical validation before reaching farms and dealers.

1️⃣

Active Ingredient Assay

High-purity raw active pharmaceutical ingredients and chelated minerals verified via HPLC and spectrophotometric assay.

2️⃣

Homogeneous Micro-Blending

Precision liquid suspension and micro-granulation processes ensuring uniform concentration in every single millilitre and gram.

3️⃣

Thermal & Stability Testing

Accelerated stability testing designed to withstand sub-tropical climatic conditions without potency degradation.

4️⃣

Field Bio-Efficacy Validation

Rigorous on-farm trial validations demonstrating improved FCR, milk yields, and zero negative residual impact.
